My story

  1. Left a Founder's Office role in April 2025, moved back home and started hunting for the next one. LinkedIn Jobs had almost nothing I wanted.
  2. The roles were being posted elsewhere. Founders putting them straight into my feed. Recruiters and HRs dropping them in comment threads under other people's posts. Nobody tracked it in one place.
  3. So I started commenting on those posts, partly to put the role in front of more people, partly to keep track of them all myself. That list became the tracker it is today.
  4. Then people started messaging me that they'd found a role through my comment, applied, and landed it. So I started sharing the list weekly on LinkedIn. Good response, more people thanking me.
  5. Weekly wasn't reaching people fast enough, so I moved it to daily. Daily takes real time and effort, so I started charging for it.

How it reaches you
